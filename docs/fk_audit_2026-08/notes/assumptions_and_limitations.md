## Layer 1: Physics of the driving-field model

1. **Poisson-gauge, scalar-only perturbations.** Enters at cosmology.tex (subsec: driving fields) and the numerical analyses declaration (cosmology.tex, "scalar-only, Phi = Psi, Born-limit path"); shift and tensor sectors are derived in the paper but dropped in the pipeline. Valid for late-time LCDM lensing where vector/tensor sources are negligible. Untested numerically; flagged as the demonstration-scenario choice in conclusion.tex.
2. **Phi = Psi (no anisotropic stress).** Enters the Poisson transfer Phi(k) = A(a) delta_m(k)/k^2 (Eq. appendix poisson) and the lensing potential Phi + Psi. Sub-percent for LCDM at the redshifts used; would need revision for modified gravity or significant neutrino stress. Untested, flagged; stated as a model assumption in the paper.
3. **Quasi-static Poisson source.** The A(a)/k^2 map ignores relativistic Poisson-source corrections; enters every leg of B_Phi (Eq. appendix bphi). Error O((H/k)^2): ~0.6% at ell ~ 10, 10-15% at ell ~ 2 (internal 2026-06-15 assessment), below the tree-bispectrum model error. Untested directly; conclusion.tex carries the explicit caveat citing clarkson2012mis.
4. **Tree-level vs nonlinear matter statistics.** Tree SPT B_delta = 2 F2 P P + cyc (Eq. appendix bdelta tree) with D(z)^4 growth (Eq. appendix bdelta growth) is the current deployed vertex; the whole rebuild exists to lift this. Expected O(1) FK shift at arcminute scales, plausibly tens of percent at wide angles (hard-pair boost tables, coverage note). Test: the rebuild itself plus Phase 3 gates (tree regression, closure test, ell-band decomposition).
5. **Equal-shell collapse (three-point Limber).** Eq. appendix equal shell approx replaces the cross-shell cumulant by its lambda-diagonal; the deployed callable is `equal_time_limber`. Error O((Delta chi/chi_s)^2), concentrated at ell <~ 10, i.e. exactly where the FK excess lives; at ell <~ 10 the collapse, not the equal-time emulator restriction, is the leading systematic (cross_shell note). Untested against a genuine cross-shell computation; flagged. Power conservation of the collapse is a property of the projection and holds for any input B.
6. **Limber per-leg ell^2/k^2 cancellation (HIGH branch).** The screen-Hessian ell^2/chi^2 cancels the Poisson 1/k^2 exactly under k = ell/chi (KAPPA3:1696-1714, 3537-3539), leaving flat-sky per-leg scalars; the exact curved-sky Wigner-3j LOW branch (ell <= 60) keeps the full kernel. Accurate for ell > 60; the discrepancy is bounded by the ell_cut = 60 seam. Test: the seam boundary probe (LOW band-difference vs HIGH at ell ~ 60, all six channels, all 16 shells, gates 1e-3/1e-1); existing self-consistency machinery `ell_band_decomp.py:110-131` (median 7e-4).
7. **Background-z assignment.** Redshift and chi are assigned along the unperturbed (Born-limit) ray, cosmology.tex (Born-limit coordinates and background redshift); the perturbed-path Jacobian is recorded but not used. Part of the demonstration-scenario slice; error enters with the other O((H/k)^2) and path-perturbation terms. Untested; flagged in conclusion.tex as future work.
8. **Near-horizon gauge terms.** The observed-redshift correction and relativistic source terms are fixed at sub-horizon background level; relevant only at ell <~ 10. Below the tree-bispectrum model error at the current budget. Untested; explicit caveat in conclusion.tex.
9. **Full driving-field transfer operator retained.** The production transfer keeps all COEFS_PHI00/PSI0 derivative terms (born_limber's dropped-term docstring describes the PyCCL reference only). Not an approximation, recorded here to prevent regressions. Test: O0 vs PyCCL agreement (0.88-1.10 across observables).

## Layer 2: B_model ingredients

1. **BiHalofit calibration domain.** Fitting formula calibrated on 41 wCDM N-body models, z = 0-3, ~10% (15%) accuracy at k < 3 (10) h/Mpc (Takahashi et al. 2020). This ~10-15% is likely the dominant error on the FK shift itself; the paper must state it without unearned validation claims. Test: pinned reference (k1,k2,k3,z) regression values (Phase 0/1); no independent N-body validation planned.
2. **BiHalofit squeezed-configuration standing.** In squeezed shapes BiHalofit is the best available formula, <= 10% vs N-body where SC01/GM12 err by > 200% (Heydenreich et al. 2023). The FK collapsed vertex is entirely squeezed, so this is the load-bearing regime. Test: squeezed-limit cross-check of models A/B against the response model C on (k_s, k_h, k_h) configurations (Phase 1 tests).
3. **baccoemu boost domain.** k in [0.017, 17.7] h/Mpc, expfactor in [0.25, 1.0] so z <= 3 (not 3.2); boost only, absolute B needs BiHalofit underneath; <2% at 68th percentile vs FLAMINGO. Outside the box the model falls back to BiHalofit or tree. Test: boost -> 1 pins at domain edges; seam boundary tests at k_min, k_max, and the z = 3 ceiling.
4. **baccoemu cold-matter conventions.** Inputs are omega_cold/sigma8_cold; with the massless-neutrino fiducial cold = total and the mapping is direct. sigma8_cold must be computed from PCAMBz0.txt, the P(k) actually fed to the build, not the 2-point side's file. Test: Phase 0 sigma8 pin note; untested beyond that (massive-nu case out of scope).
5. **Baryonification parameter degeneracy.** The boost needs 5 baryonification parameters (Mc, M1, beta, eta, theta_inn) that canoes' fiducial does not pin; Omega_b is also unpinned. These are a systematic axis, not a single number. Test: planned sensitivity quantification of the boost to the Omega_b and BCM choices (Phase 0 item 3, roadmap risk 4); currently untested.
6. **Baryonified-Nbody-not-hydro training.** The boost's truth is baryonification applied to gravity-only sims, validated against FLAMINGO, not full hydro across feedback scenarios. Residual hydro systematic inherited silently. Untested, flagged.
7. **Response-function squeezed model (option C).** B_sq = R_1(k_h) P_lin(k_s) P_NL(k_h), R_1 = 1 + G_1 - (1/3) dlnP_NL/dlnk, angle-averaged so the tidal (mu^2 - 1/3) R_K term drops; G_1 measured from separate-universe sims only to k ~ 2 h/Mpc at z = 0-2 (Wagner et al.). Exact by construction in the squeezed limit inside that domain; outside it C degrades to the tree G_1 = 26/21. Role: cross-check of A/B only, never production. Test: the A/B-vs-C squeezed comparison; G_1 public-fit availability is an open prerequisite (roadmap risk 5).
8. **Tree fallback at high z and low k.** B_tree is used below the low-k seam (exact there) and above the emulator z ceiling, where the nonlinear-to-tree ratio tends to 1 with growth suppression; at z = 3-5 even the highest-k calls are only mildly nonlinear. Fallback error dies off where the fallback is used. Test: seam agreement tests at the high-z rolloff, extreme shells z ~ 0.1 and z = 5.7 always included.
9. **Blending windows.** C^1 smoothstep in ln k / z over ~half a decade at each seam; hard clips are forbidden because they alter the k_par = 0 slice power the collapse conserves. Window shape and width are choices, not derived. Test: seam +/- delta boundary tests bypassing the dispatcher at every seam (gates 1e-3/1e-1); window-width sensitivity untested, flagged.
10. **Unequal-time lifts.** Cross-shell 2-point uses per-leg linear growth (exact) or geometric-mean P_NL (far sub-percent, Kitching & Heavens 2017). For the 3-point, the naive cube-root geometric mean has a first-order residual in time offsets precisely for squeezed shapes; the correct lift applies per-leg growth per cyclic permutation with per-term nonlinear rescaling. Not currently exercised (production is equal-shell); flagged so the rebuild never silently adopts the cube-root ansatz.

## Layer 3: Numerical and discretization

1. **GL quadrature with linear ell mapping.** HIGH branch uses n_ell = 96 Gauss-Legendre nodes mapped LINEARLY (not log) on [1e-3, 1000] for u and v, n_phi = 64, grid (96,96,64) (KAPPA3). Adequacy for the steeper boosted integrand is unverified. Test: planned n_ell re-check alongside the ell_max sweep (roadmap Phase 2); currently untested.
2. **Hard ell windows and the ell_high_max = 1000 cutoff (MEASURED 2026-08-25, see finding_ell_max_uv_sensitivity.md).** W_high = 1 iff 60 < max leg <= 1000 (KAPPA3:3548-3565). Now measured on the real vertex with the quadrature controlled: the 96-node rule IS converged at cutoff 1000 (0.1-1.1% vs 384 nodes), but at constant node density zeta(1000)/zeta(4000) = 0.12-0.88 (tree) and 0.07-0.63 (BiHalofit). The tree vertex converges slowly (decelerating exponent), the nonlinear one shows near power-law growth with exponent 1.8-2.0. This is expected: the collapsed family carries a coincident-pair moment of a field proportional to delta, which is UV-divergent, and the alpha kernel gives the hard pair no angular damping. So ell_high_max is a physical smoothing scale, not a convergence parameter. Test: the observable-level test (xi_FK at cutoff 1000 vs 2000) is specified but NOT yet run; until then FK amplitudes are conditional on ell_high_max = 1000.
3. **FFTlog Nmax (LOW branch).** LOW exact-3j uses per-leg FFTlog decompositions, Nmax = 128 over 1671 triples x 16 shells, exploiting tree separability; a non-separable B cannot enter pointwise (the separable SC01/GM12-form re-expansion is the fallback). Test: ell_cut seam probe doubles as separable-expansion fidelity check; Nmax convergence itself rests on the historical LOW self-consistency, not re-swept.
4. **Cosine grid and IDW interpolation (corner domination).** covgrid16 has 15 uniform cosines (spacing 1/7); cKDTree k=4 IDW puts >= 96% weight on the single (1,1,1) corner for exactly 27 of 40 production gammas (0.5' to 232'; >= 0.999 below 28'; the exact-hit branch never triggers), and 3.4-34.3% off-family contamination for gamma >= 294' (exceeding 10% from ~600'). Since the FK integrand carries no C lines and R depends on times only, the computed kk FK curve below ~232' is vertex-side grid-flat by construction; whether the true curve is flat is undecided. Affects the CURRENT paper FK curves. Independently recomputed 2026-08-25. Test: required grid densification with (1,c,c) rows at the 40 production c values plus a densified TREE control rebuild isolating grid effect from new physics.
5. **Lambda shell grid and the sub-396.6-Mpc zeroing.** Production queries fill ~[5.6, 2307.5] Mpc (exactly the GL-24 node span) but the table starts at 396.63 Mpc; everything below is zeroed by the out-of-range guard, dropping 6 of the 24 GL nodes (~25%), a gamma-independent systematic. Test: QUANTIFIED 2026-08-26 by folding a floor-50 rebuild against the floor-397 table at each lambda_f: -0.1% at z_s = 5, -6.6% at z_s = 0.5 at 0.5 arcmin (`theory_redshift_dependence.md` section 4 item 3).
6. **Linear-in-lambda interpolation.** The callable interpolates the 16-shell table linearly in lambda between shells. Test: MEASURED 2026-08-26 and NOT benign: the mid-path gaps carry half the z_s = 5 fold weight and linear interpolation inflates xi_FK by +11% at arcminutes (decaying through zero near 2 degrees); a truth probe at mid-gap shells certifies log-PCHIP to ~1% (`probe_lambda_interp.py`; `theory_redshift_dependence.md` section 2.2). The rebuild must densify the mid-path shells or interpolate in log.
7. **n_gauss = 24 sft-wick quadrature.** The FK fold uses a 4-D tensor-product GL integral, 24^4 nodes per call, 480 calls per production run. Convergence rests on the historical n_gauss 24/32 consistency of the production runs; no dedicated sweep with the boosted table. Test: untested for the nonlinear table, flagged; cheap to re-run at 32.
8. **equal_time aliasing.** The K vertex's three leg times collapse to one integration variable (verified bit-identical across legs by the 2026-08-25 probe); this is the code-level realization of the Layer 1 equal-shell collapse and must stay consistent with the table's `equal_time: true` meta. Test: the callable's meta guards plus the instrumented probe; regression gate is the bit-identical tree path.

## Layer 4: Software and provenance

A variant config that inherits those paths therefore deletes the deployed FK 2PCF (this happened on 2026-08-25; recovered from the `_PROD_REF_2026-06-10` copy, baseline re-verified at +4.287723e-06). Test: `run_fk_variant.py` now redirects output and both caches into the variant folder and asserts the output does not resolve inside the production directory. General rule: when reusing a driver script, its write semantics on shared paths are part of its interface and must be read, not assumed.
